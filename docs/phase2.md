# Phase 2 — Character reactions and appearance

Phase 2 adds direct, local interactions to the Phase 1 companion:

- Hovering shows a curious expression and leaving restores the selected expression.
- Clicking shows a short happy reaction. Dragging moves the creature without triggering the click reaction.
- The menu bar offers Calm, Happy, and Sleepy expressions, plus Mint, Peach, and Lavender skins. The selection persists across relaunches.
- Reduce Motion removes the brief scale reaction, while facial changes remain available. Hiding the creature stops its animation tasks and clears transient reactions.

No speech, bubbles, notifications, network access, or integrations are part of this phase.
